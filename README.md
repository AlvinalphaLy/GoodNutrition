## Core Philosophy

Calorie counting is outdated. 200 calories from whole almonds and 200 calories from a processed snack bar are not the same thing — and your body knows the difference.

GoodNutrition focuses on **food quality over food quantity**. We help users understand not just how much they're eating, but what's in what they're eating, how it accumulates over time, and what it means for their long-term health.

---

## Goals, Calories & Macros

GoodNutrition still gives you everything you'd expect from a traditional tracker — because the fundamentals matter.

Set your personal goals (weight loss, muscle gain, maintenance, or custom) and track daily calories and macros with full precision. Every meal you log contributes to a live daily summary showing your progress against your targets.

But where other apps stop there, GoodNutrition goes further. Your calorie and macro data feeds directly into your **daily and weekly scores**, giving you insight not just into _how much_ you ate, but _how well_ you ate. Hit your protein goal with whole foods? That scores differently than hitting it with ultra-processed bars loaded with artificial sweeteners.

Quantity and quality, tracked together.

## Features

### Seamless Input

Logging food should take seconds, not minutes. GoodNutrition offers three frictionless ways to track:

- **Barcode Scan** — point your camera at any product and get instant nutritional data, ingredient breakdown, and a health rating.
- **Voice-to-Log** — speak naturally: _"I had a bowl of oatmeal with berries and a black coffee"_ and the app logs it instantly. No tapping, no searching.
- **Batch Logging** — log everything you ate in one go: _"Tell me everything I ate today"_ and let the AI parse and organize it automatically.
- **Recipe & Meal Saving** — built a meal you eat regularly? Save it once, log it in one tap forever. Supports custom recipes with individual ingredients.

---

### Harmful Ingredients Intelligence

GoodNutrition doesn't just flag bad ingredients — it teaches you about them.

- **Real-Time Alerts** — the moment you scan or log a product, harmful ingredients are flagged instantly before you consume them.
- **Personal Health Profiles** — onboard with your health conditions, goals, and sensitivities. The app personalizes alerts based on _your_ profile. Diabetic? Seed oil sensitivity? Inflammatory conditions? We flag what matters to _you_.
- **Ingredient Education** — every flagged ingredient comes with a plain-English explanation of why it's harmful, for whom, and at what quantities. Not just a red label — actual knowledge.
- **Clean Alternatives** — when a product contains harmful ingredients, goodnutrition suggests cleaner alternatives that match your eating patterns and preferences.
- **Transparency Score** — every product receives a score based on ingredient quality, processing level, and how much of its label is hidden behind vague terms like _"natural flavors"_ or _"spices."_ The cleaner the label, the higher the score.

---

### Ingredient Report Card

Your most powerful weekly tool. Every week, goodnutrition generates a personalized **Ingredient Report Card** that shows:

- **Top Harmful Ingredients Consumed** — ranked by frequency and quantity across all your meals that week.
- **Clean Eating Score** — a weekly score from 0–100 reflecting the overall quality of your diet, with specific breakdowns by meal type and day.
- **Brand & Product Accountability** — exactly which brands and products were responsible for the most harmful ingredient exposure. No ambiguity — you'll know it was _that_ protein bar or _that_ sauce.
- **Swap Suggestions** — personalized alternatives based on what you actually eat regularly, not generic recommendations.

> The goal is not shame. It's awareness. You can't improve what you can't see.

---

### Grocery List & Meal Builder

Build your meals before you eat them — or plan your week in advance.

- Add individual ingredients or full recipes to your grocery list.
- Build meals directly from your grocery list items.
- The app flags harmful ingredients in your list _before you shop_, so you can make smarter decisions at the store.
- Save grocery lists as templates for recurring shopping trips.

---

### Cumulative Exposure Tracking

Single servings rarely tell the full story. goodnutrition tracks harmful ingredient exposure _over time_:

- _"You've consumed 620mg of aspartame this week across 8 products."_
- _"Your seed oil intake increased 43% compared to last week."_
- _"You've had Red 40 in 11 out of your last 21 meals."_

Cumulative exposure data feeds directly into your weekly Ingredient Report Card and your Preventive Health Score.

---

### Manufacturer Transparency Score

We believe food companies should be accountable to the people who buy their products.

- **Product Trust Score** — every brand receives a score based on ingredient quality across their entire product line, not just one product.
- **Community Flagging** — users can vote and flag products for misleading labeling, hidden ingredients, or reformulations that reduce quality.
- **Public Leaderboard** — a transparent, community-driven leaderboard showing the cleanest and worst-offending brands. Updated in real time.
- **Brand Profiles** — companies can claim their profile, see exactly why users avoid their products, and receive notifications when ingredient issues cause users to switch.

> We create direct economic pressure on food companies. When they see harmful ingredients costing them customers, they have a reason to change.

---

### Preventive Health Score & Medical Integration

goodnutrition connects your daily food choices to your long-term health outcomes — with precision, not vague warnings.

- **Disease Risk Assessment** — based on your dietary patterns, goodnutrition provides risk indicators for conditions like insulin resistance, high LDL cholesterol, inflammation, and gut dysbiosis.
  - _"Your current intake puts you at elevated risk for insulin resistance in 3–5 years."_
  - _"If you continue this pattern, expect higher LDL cholesterol at your next blood panel."_
- **Biomarker Predictions** — specific, trackable predictions that you can verify against real lab results over time.
- **Doctor Export** — generate structured, professional-grade health reports that your doctor actually wants to see. Not a screenshot — formatted data with trends, ingredient exposure history, and risk flags.
- **Progress Tracking** — as your eating habits improve, watch your Preventive Health Score improve in real time.

> We are not a medical device. Predictions are for informational and motivational purposes only. Always consult a healthcare professional.

---

### Live Grocery Shopping Assistant

Make smarter decisions before products go in your cart.

- Scan products _while you shop_ and get instant harmful ingredient alerts, transparency scores, and cleaner alternatives available on the same shelf.
- The app learns your preferences over time and proactively suggests products it knows you'll approve of.
- **Store-Level Insights** _(future roadmap)_ — aggregate data from user scans shows retailers which products are being rejected and why, creating market pressure to stock cleaner alternatives.
- **Gamification** — _"You avoided $84 worth of harmful ingredient exposure this month."_

## MVP

For the initial release and academic project submission, the MVP focuses on core functionality that demonstrates the app's unique value proposition. The following features are prioritized:

### Phase 1: Core Tracking

- **User Authentication** — sign up, login, profile creation with health goals
- **Manual Food Entry** — text-based meal logging with calorie and macro tracking
- **Barcode Scanner** — scan products to retrieve nutritional data from external APIs (Nutritionix/USDA)
- **Daily Summary** — view total calories, macros, and meal breakdown for the current day
- **Basic Harmful Ingredient Detection** — flag top 20 most common harmful ingredients (HFCS, artificial sweeteners, seed oils, etc.)

### Phase 2: AI & Intelligence

- **Voice-to-Log** — speak meals naturally and have AI parse them into structured food entries
- **Ingredient Education** — brief explanations for each flagged harmful ingredient
- **Daily Clean Eating Score** — simple 0-100 score based on ingredient quality
- **Recipe Saving** — save frequently eaten meals for one-tap logging

### Phase 3: Reports & Polish

- **Weekly Ingredient Report Card** — summary showing harmful ingredients consumed, clean eating score, and top offending products
- **Grocery List** — basic list creation from saved recipes
- **UI/UX Polish** — improve screens, fix bugs, optimize performance
- **Testing & Documentation** — final QA pass and README completion

### Out of Scope for MVP

The following features are planned for post-MVP iterations:

- Batch logging
- AI chat / adversarial nutritionist
- Cumulative exposure tracking over months
- Manufacturer transparency score & public leaderboard
- Preventive health score & medical integration
- Live grocery shopping assistant
- Community flagging & voting
